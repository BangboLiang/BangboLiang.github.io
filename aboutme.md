I'm **Bangbo Liang (梁帮博)**, a PhD student in computer science and technology at [Hunan University](https://www.hnu.edu.cn/), in the [College of Computer Science and Electronic Engineering](https://csee.hnu.edu.cn/).

I work on **high-performance networks and optical networks**, with a focus on protocol stack optimization and collective communication in optical network environments. I'm particularly interested in hardware–software co-design for high-performance data center networks and computer architecture.

## What I'm working on

- **Optical networking:** understanding optical-electrical hybrid switching and its role in intelligent computing centers.
- **Communication performance:** exploring protocol stacks, collective communication, and the details that shape system performance.
- **Tools for research:** building practical tools such as [RDMA-perftest](https://github.com/BangboLiang/RDMA-perftest), a lightweight RDMA performance testing tool.
